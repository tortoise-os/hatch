What is Hatch?

Hatch is a flash loan protocol built natively on Sui. Flash loans are uncollateralized loans that must be borrowed and repaid in a single transaction. They're used for arbitrage, liquidations, and capital-efficient DeFi operations.

On Ethereum, protocols like Aave process $50M+ daily in flash loans. Sui currently has no flash loan infrastructure - until now.
