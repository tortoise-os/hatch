## Red Flags Checklist

Monitor these weekly. If you check 3+, consider pivoting strategy:

- [ ] No new LPs in 2+ weeks
- [ ] Volume declining week-over-week
- [ ] LPs expressing concerns
- [ ] Not covering costs after Month 2
- [ ] Unable to attract more than 2 LPs
- [ ] Technical issues requiring >10 hours/week
- [ ] Competitor launched with better terms

**If 3+ red flags, pivot to:**
- Improve LP terms (higher fee share)
- Partner with a protocol for guaranteed volume
- Apply for grant to subsidize early operations
- Hire help or find co-founder
- Consider alternative business models
